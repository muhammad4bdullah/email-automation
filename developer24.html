Registrations and assignments

Round-robin uses every registration's position in ID order, including sent rows: positions 1–6 go to Members 1–6, then the pattern repeats. Sent rows keep their owner and still occupy their slot, so completing work never shifts later assignments. You can adjust pending assignments before distributing.
